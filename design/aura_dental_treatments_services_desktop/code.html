Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding appointments, insurance coordination, and biological treatment longevity.

What makes 'Biomimetic' dentistry fundamentally different from conventional fillings? expand_more
Conventional dental restorations often grind away substantial amounts of healthy tooth structure to create mechanical retention locks for rigid amalgam or large crowns. Biomimetic dentistry mimics nature: we use ultra-fine adhesive micro-layering and stress-reduced composite formulations that expand, flex, and seal exactly like natural dentin and enamel, preserving up to 80% more of your natural tooth and preventing catastrophic structural fractures.
How does the Wand® computerized anesthesia system work? expand_more
The Wand® replaces the traditional, frightening metallic syringe with an ergonomic, featherlight pen-like handpiece. A microprocessor regulates the flow rate and pressure of the anesthetic at the cellular absorption rate—below the human pain threshold. Because the flow creates a pathway of numbness ahead of the needle tip, 98% of patients report feeling absolutely no sting or pressure sensation whatsoever.
Do you accept my dental insurance, and what if I am out-of-network? expand_more
We are an out-of-network direct provider for most PPO plans, which allows Dr. Rostova to dedicate genuine 60-to-90 minute appointments rather than rushing patients through high-volume quotas. Our patient concierges process all primary insurance claims on your behalf, maximizing your reimbursement checks sent directly to your home. We also provide full HSA / FSA documentation and interest-free split payment plans.
How long do porcelain veneers and ceramic restorations typically endure? expand_more
When bonded exclusively to natural enamel using our biomimetic isolation protocols, layered feldspathic and lithium disilicate (e.max) veneers routinely demonstrate exceptional longevity exceeding 15 to 20 years. Each case includes a custom-fitted nightguard to cushion against involuntary clenching, backed by our 5-year studio restoration guarantee.
I have extreme dental phobia and haven't visited in years. What is my first visit like? expand_more
You are completely safe here. First visits for nervous patients involve zero scraping, zero treatment surprises, and zero guilt. You will sit comfortably in a quiet consultation suite, sip herbal tea, and review non-invasive 3D digital imaging at your pace. You have total agency: you can pause any examination at any second simply by raising a hand.
