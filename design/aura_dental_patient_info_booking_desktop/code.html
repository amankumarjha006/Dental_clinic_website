CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything regarding our billing ethics, insurance relationships, and sensory protocols.

What if my dental insurance is out-of-network? expand_more

Most dental PPO plans allow you to see any licensed dentist of your choice. While we are directly in-network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, and Guardian, our staff submits direct digital claims for out-of-network plans so you still receive maximum allowable reimbursement sent directly to you.

How does the $185 First Consultation pricing work? expand_more

For patients without insurance coverage, our $185 flat-fee covers full digital intraoral photography, low-radiation panoramic digital X-rays, an oral cancer screening, and unhurried diagnostic time with Dr. Elena Rostova. If you decide to pursue restorative treatments on the same visit, this fee is credited toward your care.

What if I haven't seen a dentist in years and feel nervous or embarrassed? expand_more

Aura Dental Studio was built precisely for you. We practice a strictly shame-free philosophy. Our clinicians never lecture, scold, or judge. We simply assess where you are today and build a gentle, non-overwhelming pathway forward at whatever pace feels comfortable to you.

Can I enroll in the Aura Wellness Membership immediately? expand_more

Yes! You can enroll the moment you arrive for your visit. Your benefits begin instantly with zero waiting periods, zero pre-approvals, and no annual maximum coverage caps.

What sensory comfort amenities are provided during appointments? expand_more

Every studio suite is sound-dampened and features ceiling-mounted displays with streaming nature documentaries, wireless noise-canceling headphones, warm botanical aromatherapy, weighted lap blankets, and heated lavender facial towels post-treatment.
