## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a tranquil, modern editorial health sanctuary. It rejects cold, clinical sterility and juvenile healthcare graphics in favor of an understated luxury atmosphere reminiscent of high-end wellness spaces and architectural studios. 

### Brand Personality & Core Values
- **Quiet Authority:** Confident, knowledgeable, and reliable. Information is delivered with absolute clarity, devoid of alarmist medical cues.
- **Restorative Calm:** Soft, natural surfaces and open layout rhythms actively reduce patient anxiety and foster vulnerability.
- **Architectural Precision:** Structural balance, refined typography, and purposeful white space evoke high standards of craftsmanship and modern technology.

### Aesthetic Paradigm: Modern Warm Minimalism
The visual language blends modern editorial restraint with tactile, warm hospitality:
- Generous, intentional negative space creates breathing room across all density levels.
- Precision lines and soft tints construct structured, elegant containers.
- Subtle organic accents (warm terracotta and muted sage) temper technical rigor with human warmth.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by deep, botanical teals and warm, earthen off-whites, establishing an organic balance between clinical cleanliness and natural comfort.

### Semantic Palette Architecture
- **Primary Deep Teal (`#126B64`):** Primary action items, key interactive states, active indicators, and high-impact visual anchors.
- **Secondary Dark Pine (`#173D3A`):** Structural backdrops, high-contrast badges, dark card motifs, and deep navigational containers.
- **Warm Off-White Canvas (`#FAFAF7`):** The foundational ambient background. Eliminates harsh monitor glare and provides a gentle, paper-like warmth.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated surface cards, interactive controls, dialogs, and crisp content containers.
- **Soft Mint Surface (`#E5F3EF`):** Tonal fills, selected item indicators, soft badges, and calm highlight banners.
- **Muted Sage (`#DCE7E1`):** Gentle contextual grouping backgrounds, secondary chips, and inactive timeline nodes.
- **Warm Neutral (`#F1ECE5`):** Secondary structural backgrounds, quote cards, and tactile separation bands.
- **Accent Terracotta (`#C78C69`):** Sparse focal emphasis, notification pings, premium service badging, and contextual accents.
- **Border Subtle (`#E3EAE6`):** Low-contrast architectural gridlines and surface boundaries.
- **Text Primary (`#20312F`):** High-contrast, forest-steeped charcoal for headlines and primary reading text.
- **Text Secondary (`#697873`):** Calibrated neutral for metadata, helper text, and secondary copy.

## Typography

The typography relies on **Manrope**, combining geometric balance and humanist warmth. The scale prioritizes high scannability, ample leading, and clear typographic hierarchy.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Eyebrows (`eyebrow`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em`). Used to contextualize categories, clinical suites, and procedural groupings.
- **Headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`, `headline-md`, `headline-sm`):** Set with tight letter spacing and solid line-heights. They convey quiet architectural strength rather than aggressive marketing pitch.
- **Body (`body-lg`, `body-md`, `body-sm`):** Generous line height ensures effortless legibility for complex clinical details, medical histories, and care plans.
- **Labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`):** Balanced weights tailored for buttons, form headers, navigation bars, and analytical data tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs an open, responsive grid anchored by a 12-column layout on desktop, an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapting Layouts
- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** 12 columns, fixed maximum canvas container of 1360px centered, 3rem margins, and 1.5rem gutters. Ample use of `space-xl` creates intentional breathing room between distinct procedural sections.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 2rem margins, and 1.25rem gutters. Content blocks compress gracefully from multi-column rows into 2-column or unified card arrays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1.25rem margins, and 1rem gutters. Side-by-side cards reflow into a clean vertical stream. Floating sticky bottom bars manage crucial actions such as scheduling and triage calls.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through tonal stratification and whisper-soft ambient shadows. Harsh drop shadows and heavy outlines are avoided to maintain a light, airy ambiance.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Warm Off-White (`#FAFAF7`). The foundation for page layouts and section divisions.
- **Level 1 (Flat Inset / Surface Tint):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Mint (`#E5F3EF`), or Warm Neutral (`#F1ECE5`) bounded by a hairline border (`1px solid #E3EAE6`). Used for passive content blocks, inactive calendar tracks, and treatment overviews.
- **Level 2 (Elevated Interactive Surfaces):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a dual diffuse shadow: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(32, 49, 47, 0.03), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(32, 49, 47, 0.05)`, edged with a `1px solid rgba(227, 234, 230, 0.8)`. Standard for appointment booking cards, service tiers, and doctor profiles.
- **Level 3 (Floating & Overlay Elements):** Context menus, popover tooltips, and modal sheets: `0 12px 36px -6px rgba(23, 61, 58, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 61, 58, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous curvature (`roundedness: 2`), delivering approachable elegance while preserving structured alignment.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** `0.75rem` (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`). Creates a friendly, tactile touchpoint for interaction.
- **Content Cards & Service Tiles:** `1rem` to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`). Generous rounded corners soften geometric lines and frame content cleanly.
- **Modals, Floating Panels, & Drawers:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`) along free edges, softening large structural containers.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills:** Full radius (`rounded-full`) for quick visual scanning.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Deep Teal (`#126B64`), text in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), with `0.75rem` radius. On hover: Dark Pine (`#173D3A`). Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`.
- **Secondary:** Surface Tint in Soft Mint (`#E5F3EF`), text in Deep Teal (`#126B64`), border in `1px solid transparent`. On hover: border shifts to `#DCE7E1`.
- **Outline / Minimal:** Pure White surface, Border Subtle (`#E3EAE6`), Text Primary (`#20312F`). On hover: Warm Neutral (`#F1ECE5`) background.
- **Accent:** Background in Terracotta (`#C78C69`), text in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Reserved for immediate conversions (e.g., "Book Urgent Care").

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Container:** Background in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), border in `1px solid #E3EAE6`, radius `0.75rem`. Text Primary typography (`body-md`), with placeholders in Text Secondary (`#697873`).
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to Deep Teal (`#126B64`), complemented by a soft glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(18, 107, 100, 0.12)`.
- **Error State:** Border shifts to `#C55245`, with a soft tinted background `#FDF4F3`.

### Cards
- **Standard Card:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), border in `1px solid #E3EAE6`, radius `1.25rem` (`rounded-2xl`), ambient shadow (Level 2). Internal padding: `1.75rem`.
- **Highlighted / Featured Card:** Deep Pine (`#173D3A`) surface, text in Warm Off-White (`#FAFAF7`), border in `1px solid rgba(229, 243, 239, 0.1)`. Primary button nested within adopts Soft Mint (`#E5F3EF`) with Deep Teal text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Background in Soft Mint (`#E5F3EF`), text in Deep Teal (`#126B64`), radius `rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Accent Tags:** Background in Terracotta tint (`rgba(199, 140, 105, 0.12)`), text in Terracotta (`#C78C69`), radius `rounded-full`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** `1.25rem` square, radius `0.375rem`, border `1.5px solid #DCE7E1`. Checked state: Deep Teal (`#126B64`) with Pure White checkmark icon.
- **Radio:** `1.25rem` circle, border `1.5px solid #DCE7E1`. Checked state: Deep Teal border with an interior `0.5rem` concentric teal node.

### Lists & Timelines
- Clean, unruled or hairline-divided entries (`1px solid #E3EAE6`). Icon stems or step indicators use Muted Sage (`#DCE7E1`) with active states highlighted in Deep Teal (`#126B64`).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Treatment Plan Visualizer:** Segmented card arrays featuring clear procedure sequencing, estimated recovery indicators, and cost transparency tabs.
- **Provider Bio Tile:** Square-rounded doctor portraits (`rounded-xl`), accompanied by micro-credentials in tracked eyebrow styling and a direct booking CTA.
