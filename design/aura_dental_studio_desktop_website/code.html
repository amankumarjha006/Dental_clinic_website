COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers regarding our clinical philosophy and financial policies.

What specific accommodations are offered for dental anxiety? expand_more

We specialize in accommodating nervous patients. We offer acoustic sound-isolation suites, customized streaming audio via Bose noise-canceling headsets, weighted linen blankets, pre-treatment aromatherapy, unhurried 60-minute appointment pacing, and explicit hand-signal protocols where you can pause any procedure at any time.

What should I expect during my first 60-minute visit? expand_more

Your first visit includes a welcoming herbal tea, a dedicated consultation directly with Dr. Elena, painless intraoral 3D digital impressions (zero goop or trays), low-dose radiation imaging, salivary wellness checks, and an ultrasonic hygiene cleansing. You will leave with a completely itemized, written dental plan.

Do you accept dental insurance or offer payment plans? expand_more

We are in-network with select PPO plans and file claims on your behalf for all out-of-network benefits. To maintain our unhurried 60-minute standards, we provide 100% upfront written fee schedules. For extensive restorative treatments, we partner with flexible 0% APR wellness financing options.

How do you handle dental emergencies outside standard hours? expand_more

We reserve two dedicated same-day emergency slots every weekday morning. For registered patients of the practice, our emergency triage phone line connects directly to our on-call clinician 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
